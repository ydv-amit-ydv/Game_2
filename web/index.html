THE IRON COMPACT

You command three brigades. Take the enemy keep before round twelve. That is the whole game.

1Click a brigade, pick an order, click where you mean. Then press COMMIT. Every order happens at the same moment.
2Stay in supply. A brigade more than three regions from your keep — or from a depot your engineers built — loses strength every round and dies without anyone fighting it. This is what kills people.
3Attack together. Two brigades attacking the same region in the same round arrive as one and hit much harder. One alone usually bounces off.
